🛡️ Privacy First

Privacy Policy & Data Security.

We respect your code and prompts. Aura AI is engineered around strict zero-retention principles.

1. Zero Log Retention Policy

Aura AI does not store your conversation logs, prompt histories, or generated outputs on any database. Your chats remain stored locally inside your own web browser session.

2. Key Isolation & Proxy Protection

To keep Groq API keys secure, client requests route through a stateless serverless proxy on Vercel (`/api/chat`). The proxy routes your prompt to Groq's API and returns the response without persisting data to disk.

3. Local Storage Management

Any settings, model configurations, or prompt presets saved in the app are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. You can purge this data anytime by clearing your site cookies and browser storage.
