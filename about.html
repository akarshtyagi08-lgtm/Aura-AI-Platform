💡 Human-Centric AI Engineering

Built for Speed. Designed for Humans.

Aura AI was created to strip away subscription paywalls and bloated interfaces, replacing them with raw inference speed and tactile design.

The Philosophy Behind Aura AI

Most modern AI interfaces are overcrowded with generic neon gradients, heavy paywalls, and sluggish response times. We believed there was a better way to interact with open intelligence.

By leveraging Groq's revolutionary LPU hardware and coupling it with static edge deployment via Surge.sh, Aura AI delivers instantaneous response times with zero client-side overhead.

⚡ Uncompromising Speed

Inference should feel like typing in a local terminal. We optimize every byte to ensure responses render near-instantaneously.

🎨 Tactile Aesthetics

No neon cyan or generic AI gradients. We build software with deep slate tones, micro-shadows, crisp typography, and real depth.

🛡️ Key Isolation Security

Your API keys remain completely safe. Our isolated Vercel proxy ensures frontend requests never expose secrets on client browsers.
